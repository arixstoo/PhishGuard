PhishGuard warns you at the moment you are about to click a phishing link, with a clear verdict and a one-sentence explanation.

★ The analysis runs on your computer
The detection model ships inside the extension. By default, PhishGuard sends no request at all: neither the address of the pages you visit nor the content of your emails ever leaves your device. No account, no identifier, no usage statistics, no ads.

★ What PhishGuard does
• Click the icon: the address of the open page is checked in a few milliseconds.
• Right-click a link → "Check this link with PhishGuard", before opening it.
• In Gmail: when you open a message, its links and sender are checked on your computer, and an alert appears above a trapped message. Nothing is kept or sent.
• Three verdicts, always in words and in colour: "No sign of phishing", "Suspicious: be careful", "Likely phishing".
• The reasons in plain language (a domain imitating a brand, letters from another alphabet, an IP address instead of a name…) and what to do next.
• Trusted sites: stop flagging a site you know.
• French, English and Arabic (right to left), usable with the keyboard and a screen reader.

★ Options, all turned off at installation
• Automatic check of every page you open, on your device.
• Online analysis by a PhishGuard server that you choose (your own, or one run by someone you trust): there is no default server.
• Model updates: downloaded from the project's GitHub releases, installed only if their Ed25519 signature matches the key built into the extension.
Each option asks Chrome for your permission when you turn it on, and gives it back when you turn it off.

★ Honest about its limits
No detector catches everything. PhishGuard judges the shape of an address without visiting the page: a trap hosted on a well-known platform can slip through. Keep the right reflexes: never give a password or a code received by SMS after an unexpected message.

★ Open and verifiable
Source code under the MIT licence, a model evaluated on recent data it has never seen, a published robustness report and privacy policy:
https://arixstoo.github.io/PhishGuard/
